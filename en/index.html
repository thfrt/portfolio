About
Me

Hi, I'm Thomas. Ever since I was young, I watched films and other creative projects and thought: I want to do that too. Create things, design, tell stories.

This idea became even stronger when I started teaching myself 3D design in Blender in 2019. Many more creative fields and software followed. At first purely for fun and personal projects, and later for freelance work for several clients.

I am currently studying for a Bachelor's degree in Media Informatics at Harz University of Applied Sciences. During my studies, I also developed a strong interest in film and graphic design and learned a lot about these fields. Through the degree program, I have gained knowledge across a broad range of disciplines, both theoretical and practical, creative and technical. During the last three semesters, I also had the opportunity to share my knowledge with fellow students through tutorials, including media design. I want to continue gaining experience and working on projects in these fields and everything in between.

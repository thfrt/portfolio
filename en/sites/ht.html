DaVinci Resolve, Blender

Concept, Direction, Camera, Editing, Color Grading, VFX: Isabell Schönhoff, Thomas Fritzen Assistant Director: Ronja Passauer Production Assistant: Malte Lennicke Cast listed below

Together with Isabell Schönhoff, I conceptualized and produced a trailer/promotional video for the new website of the media degree programs. The goal was to provide an overview of the study programs in a short amount of time while also generating excitement for them. To achieve this, we relied on fast-paced editing and dynamic camera movements. During production, I was able not only to strengthen my creative and technical skills but also to gain experience with larger-scale productions involving multiple stakeholders. To ensure smooth shooting days with a larger number of volunteer performers, we conducted several test shoots in advance and created detailed schedules and production plans. Since the video needed to work both on desktop and mobile devices, we had to plan for significantly different aspect ratios.

2025

Blender, Substance Painter, Illustrator, Photoshop

This camera was originally modeled for a larger scene in a university project. Since I liked the model very much, I wanted to revisit the textures and place more emphasis on the camera itself. Because I was recreating an existing object, I worked closely from technical drawings and reference images. I created most of the models in Blender using a subdivision workflow. The camera should appear visibly old and well-used, and I tried to capture that in Substance Painter.

I created the graphics for the many labels, such as focal lengths and brand markings, myself using Adobe Illustrator. I then staged the finished camera in Blender using assets from the BD3D Plant Library as well as my own photo scans.
